# 🔥 DSA ACCOUNTABILITY GUIDE (WITH LINKS, NO EXCUSES)

## 🧱 DAILY RULE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

**Every single day must have:**

1. **Learn** (45–60 min)
    
2. **Implement** (45–60 min)
    
3. **Practice** (60–90 min)
    

If even **one block is missing → day = invalid**.
